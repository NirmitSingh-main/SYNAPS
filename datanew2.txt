J. Diagnostic Assessment & Recommendation
Information-Theoretic Root Cause:
The new 10-feature tokenization directly resolved the false-positive confusion of QPSK into QAM16 (reducing QPSK $\rightarrow$ QAM16 errors from $24$ to $13$ and boosting QPSK recall from $68.00%$ to $81.33%$).
However, QAM16 under carrier frequency offset (CFO up to $\pm 50\text{ kHz}$) with Root-Raised-Cosine filtering (SPS = 5 to 20) exhibits significant inter-symbol modulation overlap within $32$-sample windows. Under standard Cross-Entropy training, the network settles on a decision boundary that favors QPSK for intermediate-SNR QAM16 signals.
Recommendation:
Keep the 10-feature tokenization architecture: It provides lower test loss ($0.4177$ vs $0.7064$), enriches the representation with local amplitude variance, and preserves circular phase integrity without increasing sequence length.
